About Hostivo

Empowering creators with hassle-free, high-performance lifetime hosting.

Our Mission

At Hostivo, our mission is simple: provide website owners, small businesses, and web developers with robust, high-speed hosting without the burden of ongoing monthly subscriptions. We believe hosting should be transparent, accessible, and dependable.

Modern Infrastructure & Technology

Our platform leverages cutting-edge enterprise hardware, featuring pure NVMe solid-state drives, LiteSpeed web server caching, CloudLinux account isolation, and cPanel account management. This architecture guarantees maximum speed, data safety, and zero bandwidth bottlenecks.

Why Choose Hostivo?

  • One-Time Investment: Pay once for your hosting account without recurring base monthly hosting bills.
  • Enterprise Speed: LiteSpeed web server and LSCache pre-configured for WordPress and PHP applications.
  • Complete Control: Industry-standard cPanel control panel with 200+ auto-installer scripts.
  • Dedicated Technical Support: Round-the-clock technical ticket support to assist with server configurations and site transfers.
